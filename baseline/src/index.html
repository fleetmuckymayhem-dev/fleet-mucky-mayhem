Welcome to

Fleet Mucky Mayhem

We are a local group of social cyclists within the Fleet (Hampshire) and surrounding areas that enjoy off-road routes - Cross Country (XC), Adventure and Exploring type of rides, frequently along the local towpaths away from the traffic. We welcome new and experienced riders, whether you have just bought a shiny new MTB/Gravel bike or dug your old bike out of the depths of the shed/garage and wiped off the cobwebs.

These are very social rides, everyone helps and supports each other. These rides are just about getting out and riding, we don't care what you ride or what you wear!

There are NO charges, NO fees, NO membership. It is TOTALLY FREE!

For details of Regular Rides, please see our WhatsApp Group .

No bike, no problem! We have a couple of suitable Loan Bikes that we lend out if you wish to try out a ride.

NOTE: We can often help with fixing/maintaining your bike to get you back in the saddle.

Frequently Asked Questions

We are based in and around the Fleet, Hampshire area of the UK.

Off-road, Cross Country (XC), Adventure, Exploring riding for fun, easy-paced (average 8-10mph), no racing!

Most rides (3 out of 4 Saturdays) depart from the Reading Road Canal Wharf car park at 09:30 sharp.

Regular Meet Point: Google Maps (Longer Rides may differ- see Ride Diary)

Depart time : 09:30 (Longer Rides may differ- see Ride Diary)

Regular Rides are 1.5-2 hours riding time and are only notified via the WhatsApp Group .

We also aim to have a social meet up on the Tuesday evening after each longer ride, again all details are shared via the WhatsApp Group only.

There are NO membership fees to join, NO charges for a ride.

YES, IT IS TOTALLY FREE TO RIDE WITH US!

Bike types:
  • Mountain (MTB)
  • Gravel
  • E-bike **
  • Hybrid
  • Cyclocross
  • Road - possible for skilled riders with appropriate tyres!

NOTE: Please wear a correctly fitted cycle helmet and have a Bell fitted, we ride on towpaths and other areas where there are walkers/joggers etc… a bell is the best way to alert them.

Depending on the time of year and the length of the Longer Rides, it is recommended to have a set of front and rear lights and certainly use front and rear mudguards when it's wet/muddy... so most of the year! LOL

** E-bike riders - Please consider the following:
  • UK legal E-bikes only
  • Your battery charge at start of the ride.
  • Your battery range.
  • If you plan to use a train to return, please check that the bike will be allowed on the train.
  • Please understand that if you run out of battery charge, it is down to you to get home.

Tyres are a very personal choice, please understand that most of the time we are NOT on tarmac.

For those new to off road riding, tyres that are more designed for off road riding will inspire more confidence...eg Mountain/Gravel knobbly style tyres

For more experienced riders they tend to know what they can ride on.

A point to note, when in the autumn/winter/spring periods ...there is likely to be more mud, hence knobbly tyres give more grip!

As always, if in doubt, please ask in the WhatsApp Group chat.

To start with… absolute no. Wear what you are comfortable in.

To improve comfort and safety, consider:

  • A helmet - riding off road there are often low branches, low bridges and other hazards. We insist that a helmet is worn for all rides. If you need to borrow one, we might be able to help. Commonsense says WEAR A HELMET
  • Padded cycle shorts - improves comfort on rides over an hour.
  • Padded cycling gloves - reduces vibration in you hands.

After that, there is endless choices!

FYI: Decathlon shop offers good quality kit for sensible prices - nearest one is in Farnborough or on-line

Our Sponsors over at Berkshire Cycle Co. Crowthorne & Woodley, will always help you out and offer discounts to the group

Although we plan to stop along the ride at a cafe, it is highly recommended you carry the following:

Fluids - 1 or 2 bottles (as large as will fit on your bike). These can be filled with water, juice, or an energy drink. If you don't have room on your bike, consider using a small backpack with a couple of bottles or a hydration pack. It is recommended that you need 500ml of water/fluids per hour of exercise.

Also, ensure you top these up along the route when you have an opportunity. Remember, if you start to feel thirsty you are already starting to dehydrate.

Foods - It is a good idea always to have some form of emergency rations to hand. These can be energy/oat bars or simple things like Jelly Babies or Haribos.

Note - that well-trained athletes can only carry up to 90 minutes of energy supplies in their bodies and you see them regularly eating and drinking.

For many years, the majority of cycling equipment was designed primarily for men. Because male and female anatomy differ significantly in key areas, women often struggled to find equipment that worked—particularly when it came to saddles.

Fortunately, women-specific clothing and saddles are now widely available and can significantly improve your riding experience. These designs are tailored to the female frame to help prevent common points of discomfort. For instance, many women's saddles feature a recessed "cut-out" running along the length to relieve soft-tissue pressure.

If anyone wants to set up a ride.

Things to be considered:

  • Design the route so that the MAJORITY of it is OFF-ROAD
  • Consider the safety of the group (not just where you fancy riding)
  • The overall distance
  • The ride time
  • Factor in stops every 15-20 mins (add 5-7 mins per stop)
  • Café stop and time to it: if over 2 hours, flag this and remind folks to carry enough fluid (500ml per hour!)
  • Opt-out points for anyone wanting to only do part of the ride, not feeling it, or having a mechanical issue
  • If not a circular route, determine how to get back and how long does it takes
  • Consider other riders and the speed:
    • We are definitely NOT racing
    • We are NOT pushing the pace
    • We have to consider new/introductory riders
Set expectations for all ahead of any ride...there should be minimal/zero surprises for folks
